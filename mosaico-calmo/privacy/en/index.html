How the app works

Mosaico Calmo is an Android word-search game. Gameplay primarily works offline and does not require an account, sign-in, or registration. We do not run a backend to sync your progress.

Data stored on your device

The app stores Campaign and Daily Challenge progress, scores, resumable games, hints used, and preferences such as language, accessibility, and reminders in private local storage. This supports gameplay and is not uploaded to our own backend. You can change preferences in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling it through Android removes local data according to the operating system; this does not necessarily delete data already processed by third parties.

Ads and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for a banner on the Home screen and an optional rewarded ad for a hint. If ads are unavailable, local gameplay remains available. Where applicable, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents consent choices. The Privacy Center explains where to find them; when available, you can revisit those choices under Settings → Privacy options.

Ad services may process and transmit to Google information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Actual use depends on configuration, consent choices, and regional rules. See the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Firebase and usage analytics

The app includes Firebase components. In the production configuration prepared for release, the app's own Firebase Analytics usage events are disabled. This does not disable advertising services or mean that no SDK processes data. See the Firebase data disclosure documentation for SDK details.

Reminders

If you enable reminders and grant the required Android permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device. You can turn them off in Settings or revoke permission in Android. The app does not operate its own remote push service.

Data the game does not request directly

Gameplay does not require your name, email address, phone number, or payment information. The game does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. This does not exclude IP address and identifier processing by the third-party SDKs described above.

Retention and choices

Local data stays on the device until it is changed or removed by you or the operating system; some Daily Challenge records may be pruned by the app's local routine. Retention and deletion of data processed by Google follow that provider's policies and controls. Available consent choices can be revisited in Settings.

Audience

The app offers a word game without social features or player-submitted content. Ads and consent options may vary by region and applicable rules; the target-audience classification is set as part of app distribution.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy if app features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact Múltiplos Digitais at multiplosdigitais@gmail.com.
